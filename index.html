This Is Anfield

The Champions League is a tournament that determines the best club in Europe, and anyone who has played or watched soccer knows it. Let me introduce a match from the year Liverpool won the tournament. In 2019, Liverpool entered the Champions League as one of the favorites to win, and despite some close calls, they made it all the way to the quarterfinals. This time, I'll be looking at the semifinal match against Barcelona. While the rules have changed slightly, the original format for Champions League matches, with teams other than the final, consisted of two matches: a home and an away game in the first and second legs. The first match between Liverpool and Barcelona was played away from home. Barcelona won 0-3 thanks to a stunning goal by Messi. This result left Liverpool fans and players in despair, and injuries to key players in other league matches added to the despair. Meanwhile, in the second leg, Liverpool supporters kicked off the game with their iconic song, "You'll Never Walk Alone." It was said that Liverpool needed to score early to win this game, and they did just that. In a tense game, a substitute scored a double to tie the score. The game-winning goal came from a kick like this, which caught the Barcelona players off guard. Knowing that Barcelona players tend to be careless on corner kicks, this was a goal from a quick restart. This match will go down in history not only for Liverpool, but for football as well. After the game, players, coaches, and fans all sang "You'll Never Walk Alone."

Miracle in Istanbul: Champions league Final

This is also about the Champions League. Liverpool's performance in recent years has been excellent, but in the generation before that, there was a long string of less than satisfactory results in Liverpool's history. Amidst this, I will talk about the final in 2005, the year they won the Champions League, as mentioned earlier. Their opponent was Italian rival AC Milan, a club that has produced many players who are now considered famous legends, and many famous players played that year as well. The first half of the match ended with a surprising 0-3 score, and everyone was probably expecting AC Milan to win. However, early in the second half, captain Steven Gerrard scored a goal, and then another goal to tie the game, and thanks to some great saves from the goalkeeper, the game went to a penalty shootout, which Liverpool ultimately won.

Video source: Liverpool vs AC Milan 2005 (YouTube)

TAA

This time, I'll be talking about a player who made me fall in love with the Liverpool team. His name is Trent Alexander-Arnold. He attended the Liverpool Academy from a young age and was recognized by the now legendary Steven Gerrard, who even called him his successor. I enjoy watching his goals, but I also enjoy watching his beautiful crosses and assists. His play is captivating, with his precise kicks, and I'm always amazed at how he made plays like that! Unfortunately, he has now moved to another team and is receiving criticism from Liverpool supporters, but I hope he succeeds and that he will return to Liverpool.
